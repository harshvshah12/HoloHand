## MODE A: HOLOGRAM CONTROL [01:05 - 02:06]
**Presenter / Lead Voice:** Bhoomi
**Category:** CATEGORY 1 — MODE A

> "Category one is Mode A: Hologram Control, dedicated to desktop automation and media presentation. An open palm represents the neutral ready state. A closed fist toggles play and pause on active operating system media like Spotify or YouTube. Thumbs up resumes playback, while thumbs down pauses. Index pointing up and down adjusts volume in discrete steps. Our flagship feature is the Optical Vernier Caliper: by simply adjusting the physical distance between the thumb and index fingertips, the system directly modulates the Windows master volume in real time, from zero percent touching to one hundred percent wide open. A peace sign toggles mute. Flicking the hand left or right skips tracks and switches browser tabs. And the rock horns gesture toggles the physical ESP32-CAM flashlight over Wi-Fi."

## MODE D: VIRTUAL AIR-MOUSE [03:24 - 04:24]
**Presenter / Lead Voice:** Harsh Shah
**Category:** CATEGORY 4 — MODE D

> "Category four is Mode D: Virtual Air-Mouse, enabling full operating system cursor and window control with zero hardware peripherals. The index fingertip maps to screen coordinates with dual-exponential smoothing and active deadzone boundaries. A quick pinch under zero-point-three-five seconds executes an anchored left click with zero cursor drift. A double pinch performs a double click. A sustained pinch engages drag-and-drop. A peace sign triggers right click. Three fingers execute middle click. Pointing the index finger up or down scrolls web pages smoothly. Our newly implemented backwards rolling-window swipe algorithm enables effortless left and right hand swipes to switch browser tabs with Control-Tab and Control-Shift-Tab. Finally, raising both hands summons the floating Holographic Command Matrix Panel."
