1. OPERATING MODES OVERVIEW & HOW TO SWITCH
================================================================================

Project HoloHand features four distinct, mutually exclusive operating modes.
Operating modes prevent accidental action conflicts (for example, taking a photo
when you only meant to change the song).

HOW TO SWITCH MODES INSTANTLY:
  - Press key '1' : Switch to Mode A (Hologram Control)
  - Press key '2' : Switch to Mode B (Camera Studio)
  - Press key '3' : Switch to Mode C (Music Conductor)
  - Press key '4' or 'M' : Switch to Mode D (Virtual Air-Mouse)
  - Or show Two-Hand Open Palm to summon the Holographic Virtual Control Panel,
    hover over any mode button with your index finger, and pinch to select!

6. UNIVERSAL GLOBAL GESTURES (WORKS IN ANY MODE)
================================================================================

1. SEQUENTIAL GESTURE (3-SECOND COUNTDOWN PHOTO):
   - Hold an Open Palm for ~0.5s, then immediately close into a Closed Fist.
   - Triggers across ALL modes: a futuristic 3-second animated circular countdown
     appears with a glowing amber arc and snaps an uncompressed photograph.

2. DUAL-HAND GESTURE (HOLOGRAPHIC COMMAND MATRIX PANEL):
   - Raise BOTH hands simultaneously in an Open Palm formation.
   - Toggles the floating Holographic Virtual Control Panel on/off.
   - Point your index finger to hover over buttons; hold for 0.75s or pinch to click.

3. OPTICAL CALIPER MASTER VOLUME GAUGE:
   - Form an optical caliper with thumb and index fingers.
   - Physical finger separation continuously sets the Windows OS master volume
     in real time (0% when touching to 100% when wide open).
   - Draws a glowing neon Vernier laser line and right-margin master audio gauge.

4. SWIPE TAB SWITCHING:
   - Flick hand left-to-right to trigger Next Tab (Ctrl + Tab).
   - Flick hand right-to-left to trigger Previous Tab (Ctrl + Shift + Tab).
   - Backwards rolling-window algorithm ensures instant sub-second recognition!

5. HARDWARE FLASHLIGHT CONTROL:
   - Make the Rock Horns sign (🤘) or Torch L-Shape (🔦) to toggle the ESP32-CAM's
     onboard high-power LED over Wi-Fi HTTP (`/flash?state=1` / `/flash?state=0`).

7. KEYBOARD SHORTCUTS & QUICK NAVIGATION REFERENCE
================================================================================

While the HoloHand application window is focused:

  [MODE SWITCHING]
    '1'        : Switch to Mode A (Hologram Desktop Control)
    '2'        : Switch to Mode B (Camera Studio)
    '3'        : Switch to Mode C (Music Conductor)
    '4' or 'M' : Switch to Mode D (Virtual Air-Mouse)

  [DISPLAY & LAYOUT]
    TAB        : Toggle between 1080p Studio Dashboard & Full-Frame Direct AR View
    'F' / F11  : Toggle Borderless Fullscreen / Windowed Mode (1280x720)
    'V'        : Cycle Visual Theme (Hologram HUD -> Cyber Scan -> Minimal)
    'D'        : Toggle Diagnostic Calibration Matrix & Tracking Coordinates

  [ACTIONS & MEDIA]
    SPACE / 'E': Toggle Desktop Actions Armed / Disarmed (Safety Lock)
    ESC        : EMERGENCY STOP — Instantly disarms all desktop automation
    'C'        : Instant Manual Photo Capture
    'R'        : Toggle Manual Video Recording Start / Stop
    'T' / 'L'  : Toggle ESP32-CAM Hardware Flashlight ON / OFF
    'P'        : Toggle Floating Virtual Control Matrix Panel
    'X'        : Toggle Dry-Run Mode (Test gestures without sending keystrokes)
    'Q'        : Clean Shutdown (Gracefully closes camera streams & threads)

8. TIPS FOR PERFECT GESTURE TRACKING & LIGHTING
================================================================================

1. Camera Distance:
   Position your hand between 25 cm to 70 cm (10 to 28 inches) from the ESP32-CAM
   lens for optimal landmark tracking.

2. Background Lighting:
   Ensure your hand is well-lit from the front. Avoid having strong direct lights
   or bright windows directly behind your hand, which can create dark silhouettes.

3. Flashlight Usage:
   The onboard flash LED is ideal in low-light environments, but in normal daylight,
   keep it OFF to prevent overexposing your hand.

4. Air-Mouse Deadzone:
   The active interaction box occupies the center 64% of the camera view (18%
   margin on each side). You only need small, comfortable hand movements in the
   center of the frame to effortlessly reach all 4 corners of your screen!
